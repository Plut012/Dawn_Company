Eval 16 — Objective areas, flags and HQs

Everything on this page is computed when it loads, from the real modules through Vite: MAPS and readMap from src/content, createMatch / resolve / viewFor from src/rules, the real board and low-poly kit, the play screen's report teller, and the real field manual in a frame. Only Vite is needed (no game server).

Not checked by the implementer: the two boards and the manual frame need WebGL. Headless Firefox in the implementer's sandbox cannot draw the WebGL board, so no screenshot was taken and the flags were never looked at by eye. Everything else on the page is text worked out by the real code, and the same facts are covered by npm test.

1. Crossroads: areas, kinds, values, HQs

A four-player match from createMatch, everything in sight. Every objective is an amber-washed area with a flag on its middle hex: a plain bone flag for nobody, the player's colour and mark for an owner. The four HQs (W X Y Z, taller flags) belong to the seats that start beside them.

2. A scripted match on the real map

Two seats on Crossroads (seat 0 Blue from the north-west, HQ W; seat 1 Plum from the south-east, HQ Y). After createMatch the three riflemen are set down by the page next to the village C and on either side of the hill E (the only thing the page does by hand), then two rounds of orders go through validateOrders and the real resolve.

  1. Round 1: Blue's 0-0 walks into the village C (alone: Blue takes it); Blue's 0-1 walks onto the hill E (alone: Blue takes it).
  2. Round 2: 0-0 walks back out of C (the flag stays Blue's); Plum's 1-0 walks onto the hill beside 0-1 (two sides in E: it stays as it was, Blue's).

3. The Flags lesson in the real manual

The real client at /?manual=flags: Blue goes into A, takes it, steps out (the flag stays), goes back in as Plum comes in too (contested: unchanged). Press Play. The Holding an objective lesson before it was rewritten for areas and flags (/?manual=score).
