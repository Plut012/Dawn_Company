Eval 18 — Supply Line

Held flags earn supply, which is the score and the one currency. A man who goes down comes back at his HQ ? rounds later, or at the end of the round his player buys him back for ? × his cost. While the HQ's flag is not his side's, nobody comes back. Part 1 needs a game server and Vite; the rest needs only Vite.

What the implementer saw (2026-11-19): the Part 1 walk-through was run in a headless Chromium against a resumed match on ports 8098/5198: 10 of 10 steps passed. That browser drew the board too: screenshots show the downed man's card with "Back in 3" and the Buy back key before the round, and after it the man standing on A1 (Ann's HQ W) with the log lines below. The fade-in at the HQ itself is an animation of about half a second and was not watched frame by frame.

1. Two real clients: lose a man, buy him back

A match is set up by the real rules (scripts/supply-log.ts: the seeded script plays two seats on crossroads until seat 0 has just lost a man it can afford to buy back) and written as the server's order log. The server resumes it; the two frames below take their seats back with the log's tokens. Every run needs a fresh log (the server appends to it). Leave 8080 and 5173 alone:

npx tsx scripts/supply-log.ts /tmp/ds18/match.jsonl        # writes the log, says who is down
npx tsx src/server/main.ts --port 8098 --timer 60 --resume /tmp/ds18/match.jsonl --matches /tmp/ds18
DS_SERVER_PORT=8098 npx vite --port 5198 --strictPort
# then open  http://localhost:5198/eval/18-supply-line.html

      The buy-back price. A seat earns about 4.2 supply a round with two players, 3.5 with three and 3.1 with four. With buyBack: 2 a rifleman (cost 2) came back for 4, about one round's income; at 4 he costs 8: 1.9, 2.3 and 2.6 rounds of a seat's income for two, three and four players. A gunner, sniper or mortar (cost 4) costs 16, four to five rounds. Objective values on crossroads were left as they were. Measured at 2, 3, 4 and 5 (seeded, 900 matches each): income per seat hardly moves (4.24–4.28, 3.46–3.51, 3.06), buy-backs per match fall from 2.2/5.1/8.6 to 1.9/3.7/5.6.

      What does not work, or was not looked at

      • The board's part of a return (the man fades in at the HQ in a ring of paper: the kit's existing sight fade and stand pulse) was seen only as its end state (the man on his HQ hex); the animation was not watched.
      • HQs are almost never lost in scripted or bot play (0, 0 and 0.01 a match; 2 in 300 bot matches), so a cut line has been exercised by the lessons, Part 2 and unit tests, not by many matches. The scripts do not try to take HQs.
      • The task doc's eval line says the man returns "the round after the HQ is retaken". The rules section of the same doc puts returns after capture in one score phase, so he is back at the end of the round in which the HQ is retaken (Part 2). The rules were followed.
      • A man who is due back this round (or is waiting for room at the HQ) cannot be bought back: buyBackCost is null, and his card says "back at round's end" with no key.
      • Buy-backs in the draft are not kept in the tab's saved draft across a reload; the server's held purchases come back in plan.purchases, so they reappear when the server holds them (it does once a draft has been sent).
      • Phone layout of the Buy back key on a squad card was not tried at phone sizes.
