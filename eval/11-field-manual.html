3. What to try

  • Play runs the whole lesson; pressed again at the end it starts over. Step plays one phase per press (phases in which nothing happens and the lesson has nothing to say are passed over); between rounds one press puts up the next round's orders; the last press brings the closing line. Restart goes back to the start, also in the middle of Play (it waits for the phase in hand to finish).
  • View as appears on the five lessons where fog matters (Line of sight, Stealth, Creeping and running, Giving the position away, Mortar team). A side sees its own orders, its own fog and its own events; the umpire sees everything.
  • Prev / Next (and the left and right arrow keys) walk the lessons in order. The lesson is kept in the URL: /?manual=cover.
  • With the game server running (npm run server), the lobby at / has a “Field manual” key above the panels, and the manual's “Lobby” key goes back; the seat is kept by its token.

5. Where it differs from the task doc, and what does not work

  • “Giving the position away” does not say what the task doc's row says. The row reads “until the next round ends”. The rules clear revealed at the start of the next resolve (first thing in the treat phase), so a unit that fired from cover is seen for the rest of that round and during the next planning, and is hidden again as soon as the next round starts to resolve. The lesson stages and states what the rules do. (The plan's own wording, “unless it fired in the last resolved round”, agrees with the rules.)
  • Notes are per phase, not per round (the Lesson shape in the task doc). In the two-round lessons a note therefore shows in both rounds and has to be true in both; in “Giving the position away” the treat note opens round 1 before anything has happened, which reads a little oddly.
  • The umpire does not keep a body on the board. The board takes any unit that is not the viewer's own off when it goes down; the umpire owns nobody, so a man who goes down falls, fades and is gone. The closing line says “down”.
  • Fog is redrawn only when a round ends. The board cannot lift the veil while it plays. In “Line of sight” the hidden man appears on a still-veiled hex for a moment, and the veil lifts when the movement step finishes.
  • Tiny maps cannot go through readMap, which demands four spawn zones of eight hexes. tinyMap in src/manual/lesson.ts reads the same three drawings (terrain, elevation, marks) with no spawns.
  • Restart, a viewpoint change or another lesson pressed during playback takes effect when the phase in hand has finished (up to a second or two); the board has no way to stop a round it is playing.
  • Changing viewpoint in the middle of a round goes back to the start of that round. So does resizing the window.
  • The board can still be dragged and pinched; it is not needed, and nothing puts the map back but Restart or a window resize.
  • On a phone the six-column maps (Sniper, Mortar team, Ambush, The five phases) are drawn at about 29 px per hex radius: readable, but the hex labels are small.
  • Opening the manual from the lobby leaves the lobby page, so the socket drops; the others see “lost signal” for that seat until the reader comes back. A ready lever that was down is for the server to keep or not; not tested here.
  • Not tried on a real phone: emulated 390 × 844 with touch events only.
