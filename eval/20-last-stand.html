Eval 20 — Last Stand

No one comes back, flags earn nothing, and the score is the men still standing. The match ends after the last round, or when one squad is left; most men win, then the dearer men, then the win is shared. Part 1 needs a game server and Vite; the rest needs only Vite. The mode is ?.

1. Two real clients, played to the end

A Last Stand match is set up by the real rules (scripts/last-stand-log.ts: the seeded script plays two Scout sections on crossroads for thirteen rounds, on a seed whose last rounds, held by both, end level on men with the dearer men deciding) and written as the server's order log. The server resumes it; the frames below take their seats back with the log's tokens, and the buttons pull each lever through the frames' own DOM for the last two rounds. Every run needs a fresh log (the server appends to it). Leave 8080 and 5173 alone:

npx tsx scripts/last-stand-log.ts /tmp/ds20/match.jsonl      # writes the log, says who wins and why
npx tsx src/server/main.ts --port 8095 --timer 60 --resume /tmp/ds20/match.jsonl --matches /tmp/ds20
DS_SERVER_PORT=8095 npx vite --port 5195 --strictPort
# then open  http://localhost:5195/eval/20-last-stand.html

      Reading the figures. The scripts and bots mostly wander and shoot at what they see; both leave a Last Stand match to its last round almost every time (one early end in 200 bot matches, none in 900 scripted), and in 15 rounds about 6 to 20 men go down, so what decides a match is usually the count at the bell, and in roughly one match in five the count is level, in one in eight to one in six picked by what the men cost. Real players who hunt will end matches early more often; none has played yet. The scripted three-player seats are unfair as ever (seat 1 wins 17% of 300, seat 0 49%; see the plan's open items).

      What does not work, or was not looked at

      • The frames play only the last two rounds, held by both: no order was given through the client's UI in this part, and the fight itself was played by the seeded script when the log was written. The orders of whole matches are exercised by the bots (real sockets) only.
      • A seat that reconnects to a finished match is sent a plan whose view is viewFor of the final state, which carries worth, so the results screen says the same; that is covered by the unit tests, not by reloading a frame.
      • The scripted and bot players almost never wipe a squad out, so "the last squads fell together" and "ended early" have been seen in the unit tests, the property test and Part 2 here, not in many whole matches (0 in 1100).
      • Last Stand has no HQ line to hold: the HQs are still on the map as flags that earn nothing, and a squad that loses its HQ loses nothing. The play screen still draws them. Nothing in the task asked for them to go.
      • The game log strip says "down at X. For good." for a man going down; the "Standing" tally per round is not told in the strip (there is no event for it), only in the header and the lamps.
      • The phone layout of the results screen's extra sentence was not tried.
